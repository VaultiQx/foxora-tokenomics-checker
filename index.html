Questions

Why can I not see an allocation?+

Allocations are holder-only. If the wallet you checked holds no Foxora NFT on Robinhood Chain, there is nothing to show. If you bought a fox with a different wallet, check that wallet instead.

How many tokens does one fox get?+

45,005 FOXORA per fox. The holder pool of 100,000,000 FOXORA is split evenly across the 2,222 foxes, and every extra fox in the same wallet adds 45,005 FOXORA again.

When can I claim?+

At the token launch. The date and the claim steps will be announced in the Telegram community and on X before claiming opens. Keep your fox in your wallet until then.

Is checking safe?+

Yes. The checker only reads the public Foxora balance of the address you paste, straight from Robinhood Chain. It never asks you to connect a wallet, sign anything, or approve a transaction.
